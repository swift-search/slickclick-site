What it is

Slick Click is a Chrome extension. Highlight text on any page and a small menu appears: search it across the sites you choose, define it, translate it, summarize it, save it for later, or ask a question about it.

It works on every website, inside PDFs, and is completely free.

How it differs from most extensions

A free extension is usually free because you're paying some other way. Slick Click has no ads, nothing sold and no account needed for anything but the AI, and your sites, settings and highlights never leave your machine.

Who makes it

Slick Click is run by a tiny but dedicated team who are continuously working hard to improve the extension.
